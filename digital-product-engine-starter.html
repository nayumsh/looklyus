Creating an Etsy Digital Product Is More Than Making a Template

Selling a digital product can involve many separate tasks before it is ready to go live.

  • Decide what to sell
  • Create the actual product
  • Prepare product variations
  • Create listing images
  • Create a listing video
  • Write the Etsy title
  • Research/use tags
  • Write the description
  • Prepare the listing for upload

DPE Starter brings these pieces together into one workflow.

Pick → Build → List

Pick

Choose an idea from supported product categories and use the included planning resources to understand the audience and product opportunity.

Build

Create the working digital product in formats such as XLSX, CSV and Notion.

List

Prepare listing photos, a short video, title, 13 tags and product description.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is DPE Starter?

DPE Starter is software from The Elite Engines (Anil Krishna & Dave Espino), designed to help create a finished Etsy listing kit using a Pick → Build → List workflow.

What does DPE Starter create?

A working spreadsheet product (XLSX, CSV and Notion), 8 listing photos, a short listing video, and Etsy copy: title, 13 tags and a full description.

Is this for Etsy beginners?

It is positioned for beginners as well as existing sellers. The DPE Core Five includes 5 short guides with PDFs and a 60-idea planner to help beginners decide what to create.

What product categories are included?

Budget, Debt Payoff, Meal & Grocery, Fitness, Habit Journal, Wedding Planner, Moving Planner and Content Calendar.

What formats are supported?

The working product can be created as XLSX, CSV and a Notion version.

Does it create Etsy listing copy?

Yes. It is designed to create an Etsy title, 13 tags and a full product description.

Does it create listing images?

Yes. It is designed to create 8 listing photos for each kit.

Does it create a listing video?

Yes. It is designed to create a short listing video.

Will DPE Starter guarantee Etsy sales?

No. DPE Starter does not guarantee sales or income. Results depend on factors such as product quality, pricing, Etsy demand, competition, listing optimization and marketing.
